## Why no animation was compiled from these images

The required result is one coordinated person moving with stable support and identity. Current outputs are mostly several hand poses on a nearly fixed head/chest, with uncontrolled gesture distance. A source/hash inventory, removal of the background, more FPS, crossfades, or forcing pixels outside a mask to match would not establish that result. No normal-speed visual pass can honestly be claimed from this rejected trajectory. A finite review animation was therefore not assembled by pretending the mismatched sequence was continuous.
