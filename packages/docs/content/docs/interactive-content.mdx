# Interactive content & quoting

Conversations in Varin go beyond plain text — you can interact with structured options, inspect live charts, and quote exact passages or tool results back into your next prompt.

## Inline controls and HTML artifacts

- **Inline controls**: Assistants can embed single/multi-select choices, forms, tabs, disclosures, and live links to tasks, files, or plans right between paragraphs. Selecting an option prepares an editable message in your composer rather than sending behind your back.
- **Interactive HTML/JS**: Complex visualizations, calculators, and comparisons render inline inside an isolated sandbox matching your Varin theme. Right-click (or use the menu button on touch devices) to inspect source, expand to the sidebar, download a standalone HTML copy, reset state, or quote the current view.

## Precise quoting and draggable markers

- **Quote anything**: Highlight text across paragraphs, right-click a specific block (code, table, paragraph), pick **Quote full turn**, or quote a tool call, image, or interactive view.
- **Composer markers**: Quotes and images appear as compact `[Quote N]` and `[Image N]` chips that you can drag to reorder inside your message, inspect on click, or undo as one edit. Removing a marker from text leaves the attachment available in the attachment bar; removing the attachment clears all its markers.
- **Image annotation**: Click any draft or chat image to draw arrows, shapes, text, numbered callouts, or opaque redactions. Redactions are burned into the sent image at original resolution without attaching the unredacted original.
