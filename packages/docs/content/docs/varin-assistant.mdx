# Varin assistant

**Varin** is your long-lived personal assistant view. Talk through ideas, hand off tasks to specialized agents, keep chatting while work continues, and come back later with your context and memory intact. You can keep multiple Bots with separate instructions, models, memory, schedules, and appearances.

## Keep talking while work runs

- By default, sending a follow-up message in a Varin conversation steers the ongoing turn once the current step finishes. Use **Alt+Enter** (or the queue button beside Send) to queue a message for after the current turn.
- Open **Work details**, **Files**, or **Computers** in the side tabs beside your conversation without leaving the chat. Closing a side tab never cancels running work.
- One assistant can keep multiple conversations and branches that all share the same profile, home directory, memory, and schedules.

## Group chats

- Start a group chat from the Bot sidebar to let several Bots collaborate on one shared timeline.
- Mention (`@`) one or more Bots, or reply to a Bot's message, to continue with those specific members. Unaddressed messages only reach members with listening enabled; if nobody is listening, the post stays in the group without starting a turn.
- Share files and search past messages directly in the group. Bots can read group history on demand and address other members. The side panel brings together members, activity, and group schedules using the same Calendar and follow-up controls as direct conversations.

## Memory and matters

- Open the **Memory** tab in the right panel (or open **Settings → Bot** and switch to **Memory**) to inspect, edit, organize, or forget what a Bot remembers across conversations.
- Original chat history stays untouched when you forget a memory; forgotten material is simply excluded from future model context.
- **Matters** track ongoing topics and link directly to the underlying delegated tasks so you can jump into the full [Workbench](/views-and-collaboration/) whenever you want closer control.

## Schedules and event watches

Open **Schedules & Watches** in the right panel to manage recurring schedules and incoming event watches from connected plugins:

- Choose how incoming events are handled: **immediately**, **batched** over a window, on a **schedule**, or **queued** for your next turn.
- Pause, resume, or trigger **Process now** on any watch without deleting its history.

## Profiles, appearance, and desktop companion

- Open a Bot's profile from its side panel, sidebar, or **Settings → Bot** to edit its instructions, model, thinking level, memory, and appearance in one place.
- Under **Settings → Bot → Profile → Change appearance**, choose from **16 built-in animated characters** (including Varin) or import custom images and animation packs. When creating a Bot, leave the appearance on **Random** to let Varin pick, and leave the name blank to use the chosen character's name.
- On the desktop app, open **Settings → Companion** to download an optional desktop companion that follows your selected assistant's activity. Putting the companion to sleep closes only the character window and does not pause your assistant.
